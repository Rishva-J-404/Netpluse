# NETPULSE Project Plan & Roadmap
**Course:** Probability and Statistics for Computer Science (25CS203)
**Institution:** Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences

## 1. Objective
Build an interactive educational Network Operations Center (NOC) and probability laboratory utilizing Gaussian Normal Distribution, standardized Z-score deviation, SciPy cumulative probabilities, and empirical anomaly detection.
